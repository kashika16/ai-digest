I am submitting **Option 1: Executive Proposal** for my final course project. My project is **AI Daily Digest**, a working AI intelligence product that sends a curated morning email summarizing the most relevant AI developments.

The submission focuses on the leadership problem the product solves for me and the people I work with: too much AI information, not enough decision-ready signal. I built it to stay current, avoid feeling behind, apply relevant AI developments more quickly in my own projects, and share higher-signal updates with my team and peers. The intended workflow is to review the digest in email before starting work and use the most relevant items in projects and team discussions. In the memo, I explain the business problem, the proposed outcome, the operating and technology implications, the economic logic, the tradeoffs, and the 90-day path forward.

The artifact itself is usable beyond the course: I built a functioning system that curates selected sources, filters for high-signal updates, rewrites items into key points with relevance for real work, and distributes the digest on a recurring schedule for personal use and lightweight sharing with my team and peers. I am also including a public GitHub repository and simple local demo instructions.
